ramaiah hardware org

ramaiah hardware org, or rho 𝜌 for short, aims to be the first electronics club of its kind in RIT. we explore free and open source tools for all kinds of hardware design, from device physics simulation to IC design to PCB design, and much more. our mission is to democratize hardware design -- incubate an environment for creative design unrestricted by licenses, NDAs and paywalls.
