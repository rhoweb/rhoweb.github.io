about us

ramaiah hardware organization, or rho 𝜌 for short, is a student-run hardware design club at RIT focused on the open-source EDA ecosystem — spanning analog design, RTL design and verification, physical design, and the emerging technologies reshaping what silicon can do. our mission is to democratize hardware design — to incubate a creative environment where innovation isn't deterred by expensive licenses or institutional access.
